## 01 / Operating Context

# Production pressure needs a governed response.

Architecture practices often face tight documentation schedules and shifting design requirements. Production support scales capacity without compromising office standards or architectural intent.

### Typical Buyer Pressures

- Compressed submission and documentation programmes
- Uneven internal Revit or visualization capacity
- Design changes propagating across models, sheets and media
- External production that does not follow office standards

### Production Requirements

- BEP- and template-aligned authoring
- Documentation coordination against approved design information
- Planning and stakeholder visuals tied to named revisions
- Transparent assumptions, issues and structured handoff
