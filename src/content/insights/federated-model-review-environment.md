## Control the inputs first

Federation starts with version, origin, coordinate and suitability checks. A clash result produced from stale or misaligned inputs creates noise rather than coordination value.

- **Discipline and revision register**
- **Coordinates and units**
- **Agreed clash rules and tolerances**
- **Excluded systems and known limitations**

---

## Issue identification is not resolution authority

Issues can be grouped, described, assigned and tracked by a production partner. Design decisions and closure authority stay with client-authorized discipline leads.

- **Named issue owner**
- **Decision status and due date**
- **Approved model-change instruction**
- **Re-test result**

---

## Close with a structured record

The handoff should pair the federated package with open/closed issues, decisions, assumptions and the exact source versions reviewed. This maintains traceability without overstating clash-free or compliance outcomes.
