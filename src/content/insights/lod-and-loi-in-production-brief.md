## Start with intended use

LOD and LOI only become operational when linked to a defined use, exchange milestone and responsible approver. A stage label alone does not define geometry, information or reliance.

The production brief should identify which objects, parameters and outputs matter for the package, alongside explicit exclusions.

- **Intended use and recipient**
- **Authoritative source revision**
- **Geometry and information threshold**
- **Exchange format and validation method**

---

## Separate production from approval

Axys3D can translate agreed requirements into authoring tasks and model checks. The client and appointed professionals remain responsible for approving the governing requirements and accepting the issued information.

- **Name the requirement owner**
- **Record assumptions and deviations**
- **Route decisions through client-led approval**
- **Issue a limitations and status register**

---

## Make handoff testable

A structured handoff should let the receiving team confirm what was produced, against which inputs, at what status and with which unresolved items. This is more useful than an unqualified LOD label.
