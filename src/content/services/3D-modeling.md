## 01 / Information baseline

# Controls before production.

Obtain reliable, reusable geometry for downstream BIM, visualization, animation and real-time workflows.

### Required inputs

- Approved drawings or reference models
- Survey or scan data where applicable
- Target use, units and coordinate system
- Geometry, naming and optimization requirements

### Naming and controls

- Units, origin, axes, coordinate system and reference elevation
- Object, layer, collection, component and material naming
- Source-revision, derivative, ownership and approval status
- Geometry tiers, instancing and reusable-asset conventions

### Standards and constraints

- Target use determines geometry and information thresholds
- LOD/LOI terms use project-agreed definitions rather than assumed stage mappings
- Survey or scan gaps, inferred geometry and exclusions are explicitly logged
- Downstream performance budgets and required editability are agreed before optimization

### Verified software and formats

- Project-agreed native source files supplied by the client
- IFC where the approved source or handoff requires structured BIM exchange
- Project-agreed geometry exchange format and version recorded before use
- A format/version register accompanies every issued derivative

## 02 / Scope and gates

# Production stays reviewable.

Axys3D progresses work against named inputs. Design-bearing decisions and approvals remain client-led.

### Technical scope

- Architectural and contextual modeling
- Conversion, cleanup and retopology
- LOD/LOI-aligned production where applicable
- Reusable asset and material preparation

### 01 — Validate source coverage and target use

**Review gate:** Source gate: coverage, scale, coordinate basis and intended use accepted.

### 02 — Define geometry and information thresholds

**Review gate:** Sample gate: representative geometry, naming and information level approved.

### 03 — Model and check representative samples

**Review gate:** Completeness gate: scope and logged assumptions reviewed.

### 04 — Complete production with staged reviews

**Review gate:** Optimization gate: target derivative passes agreed geometry checks.

### 05 — Optimize and package downstream variants

**Review gate:** Handoff gate: format, version, limitations and ownership acknowledged.

## 03 / Delivery definition

# Acceptance is part of handoff.

### Deliverables

- Purpose-built 3D models
- Cleaned or converted geometry
- Reusable component library
- Optimized downstream assets

### Acceptance criteria

- Scale, origin, orientation and coordinate basis match the agreed reference
- Geometry completeness and exclusions are traceable to the scope
- Naming, hierarchy and material assignment support the stated downstream use
- Client approver accepts the model and limitations register

### Structured handoff

- Native and exchange formats
- Scale, origin and coordinate note
- Known limitations register
- Asset naming and version record

### Evidence required

- Written scope approval
- Current source-information register
- Named client approvers
- Approved publication evidence

## Responsibility boundary

Models follow agreed inputs and intended uses. Fitness for statutory, construction or professional reliance requires client-led review and approval.

Axys3D provides production, modeling, coordination or delivery support as scoped. It does not act as architect or engineer of record and does not provide statutory sign-off.

## Technical FAQ

# Scope questions, answered precisely.

### Can models be used for BIM and visualization?

Where requirements are agreed early, a governed source can feed distinct BIM, visualization and real-time derivatives.

### How is LOD/LOI handled?

Only project-agreed definitions are used; no universal equivalence between stages or standards is assumed.

### Are models suitable for statutory issue?

Only after review and approval by the responsible client-appointed professionals.

### How is geometry accepted for downstream use?

Against agreed scale, origin, hierarchy, naming, scope completeness and the geometry threshold for the named BIM, visual or real-time derivative.
