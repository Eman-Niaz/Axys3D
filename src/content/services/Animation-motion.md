## 01 / Information baseline

# Controls before production.

Turn spatial and technical information into controlled motion outputs for launches, presentations and stakeholder communication.

### Required inputs

- Approved geometry and information freeze
- Audience, runtime and narrative brief
- Camera and brand direction
- Delivery specifications and rights approvals

### Naming and controls

- Sequence, shot, camera, frame range, version and approval status
- Source-model and information-freeze register per affected shot
- Review proxy, master and channel-derivative conventions
- Ownership and rights record for music, voice, type, footage and graphics

### Standards and constraints

- Runtime, frame rate, aspect ratio, resolution and delivery channel are agreed before production
- Design changes are assessed against every affected shot and production stage
- High-cost sequence production begins only after storyboard and camera approval
- Third-party media is excluded unless rights and usage are documented

### Verified software and formats

- Approved geometry and imagery from the project-agreed source workflow
- Review proxies and delivery masters in client-agreed codec/container specifications
- Frame sequences where required by the approved post-production workflow
- Shot, revision, rights and delivery registers accompany handoff

## 02 / Scope and gates

# Production stays reviewable.

Axys3D progresses work against named inputs. Design-bearing decisions and approvals remain client-led.

### Technical scope

- Story and camera planning
- Walkthrough and sequence production
- Motion-graphics integration
- Editing, sound-placement and mastering support

### 01 — Lock narrative, runtime and review owners

**Review gate:** Narrative gate: audience, runtime, message and review owners accepted.

### 02 — Approve storyboard and camera blocking

**Review gate:** Storyboard gate: sequence, camera and visible information approved.

### 03 — Develop look frames and test sequence

**Review gate:** Look gate: representative frame and motion treatment approved.

### 04 — Produce staged animation reviews

**Review gate:** Sequence gate: edit, timing and design-bearing content approved.

### 05 — Master approved versions and derivatives

**Review gate:** Master gate: rights, credits, format and channels authorized.

## 03 / Delivery definition

# Acceptance is part of handoff.

### Deliverables

- Storyboard and camera plan
- Animation sequences
- Motion-graphics package
- Approved delivery masters

### Acceptance criteria

- Runtime, aspect ratio, frame rate and master specification match the brief
- Approved shots reference the named information-freeze revision
- Shot, change, rights and approval records are complete
- Client authorizes the master and named derivatives for intended use

### Structured handoff

- Master and channel variants
- Shot and revision log
- Source assumptions record
- Rights and credit schedule

### Evidence required

- Written scope approval
- Current source-information register
- Named client approvers
- Approved publication evidence

## Responsibility boundary

Animation represents approved source information at a stated revision. Design, compliance and publication approvals remain client-led.

Axys3D provides production, modeling, coordination or delivery support as scoped. It does not act as architect or engineer of record and does not provide statutory sign-off.

## Technical FAQ

# Scope questions, answered precisely.

### When should the design be frozen?

Before high-cost sequence production. Late changes are assessed against affected shots and schedule.

### Is streaming included?

No. Milestone 4 includes production-page structure only; hosting and streaming infrastructure are deferred.

### Can client music or media be used?

Only with documented rights and usage approval.

### How are late design changes handled?

The revised source is compared with the locked information freeze, affected shots are identified and schedule/cost implications are agreed before work resumes.
