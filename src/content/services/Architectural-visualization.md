## 01 / Information baseline

# Controls before production.

Keep visual communication aligned with the current design state, audience and approval purpose.

### Required inputs

- Approved model or drawing set
- View list and communication objective
- Material, landscape and context references
- Brand and disclosure approvals

### Naming and controls

- Source-model, drawing and view-brief revision register
- Camera, option, image, channel and approval-status naming
- Material, landscape, entourage and context source ownership
- Named technical reviewer, visual approver and publication approver

### Standards and constraints

- Each image is tied to a stated design-information freeze
- Planning, technical and marketing uses are distinguished in the brief
- Unverified context, materials or design assumptions are disclosed for review
- Publication requires approved project identity, rights, credits and client permission

### Verified software and formats

- Client-approved model and drawing inputs in the agreed source version
- High-resolution still-image masters in a project-agreed raster format
- Channel-ready derivatives generated from an approved master
- A source, revision and rights register accompanies the final set

## 02 / Scope and gates

# Production stays reviewable.

Axys3D progresses work against named inputs. Design-bearing decisions and approvals remain client-led.

### Technical scope

- Exterior and interior CGI
- Planning and approval visuals
- Design-option and stakeholder studies
- Marketing imagery after rights approval

### 01 — Confirm information freeze and view brief

**Review gate:** Brief gate: audience, purpose, views and source freeze accepted.

### 02 — Audit and prepare source geometry

**Review gate:** Camera gate: composition and visible scope approved.

### 03 — Develop camera, material and lighting direction

**Review gate:** Look gate: representative materials, lighting and context approved.

### 04 — Run staged technical and visual reviews

**Review gate:** Technical gate: design-bearing information reviewed by the client.

### 05 — Issue approved masters and archive sources

**Review gate:** Publication gate: final image, rights, credits and channels authorized.

## 03 / Delivery definition

# Acceptance is part of handoff.

### Deliverables

- High-resolution still imagery
- Approval-stage visual sets
- Option comparison views
- Channel-ready derivatives

### Acceptance criteria

- Views and visible scope match the approved camera schedule
- Design-bearing content references the named source revision
- Known assumptions, image edits and excluded information are recorded
- Client approves the masters and intended publication channels

### Structured handoff

- Approved masters
- View and revision register
- Source assumptions log
- Usage-rights record

### Evidence required

- Written scope approval
- Current source-information register
- Named client approvers
- Approved publication evidence

## Responsibility boundary

Visualization communicates client-approved information; it is not a substitute for statutory documentation, certification or professional design approval.

Axys3D provides production, modeling, coordination or delivery support as scoped. It does not act as architect or engineer of record and does not provide statutory sign-off.

## Technical FAQ

# Scope questions, answered precisely.

### Can imagery support planning submissions?

It can support planning communication, but Axys3D does not claim permit approval or statutory acceptance.

### How are design changes controlled?

Each review is tied to a named source revision and client-led approval gate.

### Can the work be published?

Only after client, project and asset rights are explicitly approved.

### How do technical and visual approvals differ?

Client technical reviewers approve design-bearing content and source alignment; the named visual approver accepts composition and treatment; publication is a separate rights gate.
